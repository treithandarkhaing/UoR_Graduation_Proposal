Budget basis: The proposal is calculated for 200 guests: 60 graduating learners, 30 special guests and staff, and approximately 110 guardians.

Alternative attendance scenario: If the company allows only one guardian per graduate, the university-covered attendance may be reduced to approximately 150 guests (60 graduating learners, 60 guardians and 30 special guests/staff). The remaining estimated 50 guardians would pay for their own catering, reducing the total university cost by approximately USD 1,000.

Catering is calculated at the venue's per-pax rate × 200 guests. A 5% contingency is added for décor, signage and miscellaneous expenses. Hotel rates use the Myanmar government exchange rate; other suppliers may use the prevailing market exchange rate for USD payments.

Graduation Gown

Gown rental programme for 600 graduating students — rental, refundable deposit and damage liability are listed below for approval.

💳 Rental Fee

MMK 60,000per gown / set

Includes cap, hood and university sash

👗 Deposit Fee

MMK 50,000per set (refundable)

Collected at fitting, refunded on return in good condition

🛡 Damage Fee — Minor

MMK 50,000per set

Loose stitching, small stains, missing buttons

⚠️ Damage Fee — Major

MMK 100,000per set (replacement)

Torn, burned or unrepairable — charged at replacement value

⚠️ Terms & Conditions

  • Deposit is fully refundable when the gown is returned undamaged within 7 days after the ceremony.
  • Minor damage (loose stitching, small stains, missing button) — flat repair fee of MMK 50,000 per set.
  • Torn or unrepairable sets are charged at replacement value of MMK 100,000 per set (deposit + rental are not offset).
  • Fitting sessions will be arranged 2 weeks before the ceremony at Theigi Shwe Yi.
